At guest time ~38320.919, original kernel logged `**UIPC ERROR** free_dynamic_msgq_id: QID[808261c0] M=1 C=0 S=0 A=1`. Later the original OSD allocation deadline diagnostic printed `FX3_OSD_ALLOC_DEADLINE_EVIDENCE_END diagnostic_only=1`; the runner's existing explicit diagnostic gate stopped the run. This is the next precise lifecycle blocker to review, alongside the two >5000 ms bounds. Do not suppress the gate or raise the original guest timeout to obtain a green run.
